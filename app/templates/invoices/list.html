What is this page?

The Invoices page lists all invoices in the system. You can filter by status, view details, and manage invoices.

Status meanings:

  • Pending — awaiting review or analysis
  • Flagged — anomalies detected, needs attention
  • Approved — reviewed and cleared for payment
  • Rejected — rejected due to issues

How to use:

  • Click an invoice number to view its details and anomalies.
  • Use the status filter to narrow results.
  • Click “New Invoice” to add one manually.
