What is this page?

The Dashboard provides an at-a-glance overview of your accounts payable invoices and detected anomalies.

Key metrics:

  • Total Invoices — all invoices in the system
  • Pending — invoices awaiting review
  • Flagged — invoices with detected anomalies
  • Open Anomalies — unresolved anomaly findings

How to use:

  • Click on an invoice to view its details and anomalies.
  • Use the sidebar to navigate to other sections.
