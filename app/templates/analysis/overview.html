What is this page?

This page shows all detected anomalies across your invoices. Use it to review, prioritize, and resolve findings.

Severity levels:

  • Critical — requires immediate attention (e.g., amount 5x+ above average)
  • High — significant anomaly, likely needs investigation
  • Medium — moderate deviation, review recommended
  • Low — minor flag, informational

How to use:

  • Click “Analyze All Pending” to scan all pending invoices.
  • Click on an invoice link to see full details.
  • Resolve anomalies after reviewing them.
